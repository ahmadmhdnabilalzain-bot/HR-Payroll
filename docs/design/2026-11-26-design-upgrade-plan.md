# Design upgrade plan (PROVISIONAL)

Status: provisional. Made with the advisor (Fable 5.2) on 2026-11-26.
Specifics are marked provisional until the competitor reports are read
in full and the owner has picked reference screens.

## The owner's standard (verbatim, 2026-11-25/26)

"Extremely advanced with motion graphics and animation, extremely
smooth, easy user interface and navigation, looking super high tech,
bright and dark mode switches, no long side bar menus, the flow of the
functions need to be clear and doesn't confuse the staff. It shouldn't
look like it was made with AI. I genuinely want clients to be amazed."
Round-2 feedback: the three concepts were "childish and definitely not
professional"; the competitors must be checked first.

## Steps

1. Finish the evidence: read both benchmark reports in full; check
   coverage of all 11 UAE vendors (StackHX has no capture yet), desktop
   product screens and motion; fill gaps from official walkthrough
   videos. Logged-in competitor screens cannot be captured; ask the
   owner for demo access or screenshots.
2. Owner calibration (the gate): a contact sheet of captured screens;
   the owner names 2 or 3 that feel right and which feel wrong.
3. Write the standard: the brief clause by clause, each with a check
   that can be run; add "childish, not professional"; measure
   smoothness on real hardware, not software rendering.
4. Evidence rule: every visual or motion element traces to a reference
   the owner picked or a clause of the brief. Round-2 elements no
   reference uses in a product (particle flows, custom cursor,
   hold-to-approve) stay out unless the owner's picks show otherwise.
   "Motion graphics, high tech, amazed" stays unresolved until step 2.
5. Build one screen pair, not four flows: owner home plus one payroll
   step; both themes, English and Arabic, desktop and phone; numbers
   from `packages/demo-data`.
6. Review beside the picked references by a fresh reviewer before the
   owner sees it.

## Patterns seen so far (global phone apps only; not competitor findings)

Home leads with items needing a decision, each with an inline button;
one large carefully set number; visible step progress; loud colour in
marketing, quiet colour in the app; a floating pill for navigation.
