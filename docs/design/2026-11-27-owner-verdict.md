# Owner verdict, 27 Nov 2026 (every point, none dropped)

Source: the owner's messages today. This list is the contract. Each item is
a job a real person must be able to finish in the app. "Done" means a cold
test passes: the person types nothing the system already knows, never picks
an approver, and finds it in three taps.

## From the long message

1. **Role and login.** "How can I log in as an employee and then request a
   role?" The role switch lets anyone become anyone. In the product everyone
   has one identity with fixed permissions; approvals and audit are tied to
   the real person. The switch must read as a presenter control ("Demo: view
   as"), separate from the product.
2. **HR Home shows one bank issue, titled very big.** HR has many priorities.
   Home must show, ordered by urgency: staff KPIs; staff schedules;
   attendance; visa and labour-card documents; who has expired insurance;
   government letters and issues; requests issued by staff. Not "kept or
   designed properly" today.
3. **Staff schedules.** "I didn't find the function where the staff schedules
   are." Rosters/schedules must exist and be findable.
4. **In-app communication instead of email.** Staff open a chat with another
   staff member. Avoid email between staff as much as possible.
5. **GM: what every staff is doing today.** Each person's task list and the
   deadline for each task. "A big turn-up to lead in the market."
6. **Operations app, not only HR and payroll.** The logic of company
   operations, for any company (bank or not). Approvals and audit linked so
   the module works properly.
7. **Org chart.** Each person sees who they report to. Responsibilities per
   position.
8. **KPIs, company guidelines, grades.** Grades in the company (example: a
   manager is 5C). Starting level depends on position. Every staff labelled
   with their grade next to their name.
9. **Leave requests.** Maternity and every other leave type. An employee must
   be able to request them.
10. **Salary certificate and signing authority.** The employee must not pick
    who signs. Each letter type has a defined authority, even in a demo at
    least the HR manager; a two-step request (HR manager, then GM) is
    expected.
11. **Letter templates.** Many letters: bank letter, visa/embassy letter,
    proof of employment, and more. The employee raises a request and types
    nothing: his data is in the system. He only selects the embassy, the
    purpose, the bank from drop-downs.
12. **Competitive study.** Browse competitors' navigation and features
    (Keka, Zoho, Odoo and others). "Not better than any competitor." At
    USD 10,000 a year a bank would not buy this. It must be much more premium
    and operational; the experience is "very basic".
13. **Many things missing**, not only a features list: logical operation.

## From the latest message

14. **Reports section is dead.** As HR manager, Reports shows tabs and nothing
    is clickable. Every report must be openable.
15. **Official PDF.** Every report and document opens as a PDF with the
    company letterhead, logo and everything: an official document. Every type
    of report must be generatable as a PDF straight away, not only seen on
    screen.

## Earlier today, still open

17. **Premium bar.** 10/10, "Apple is developing this app"; motion and
