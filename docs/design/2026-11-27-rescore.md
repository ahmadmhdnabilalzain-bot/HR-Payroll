# MubinFlow rescore after the ink-and-grey round

Date: 2026-11-27. Reviewer: visual design critic. This is a read-only
critique: no product code was changed. It rescores branch
`claude/quirky-wozniak-b7xkvr` at dce478f against
`2026-11-27-final-critique.md` (7.3) and
`2026-11-26-apple-standard-critique.md`.

The bar is the owner's: 10/10, as if Apple built it. The palette is ink,
paper and grey only, and colour appears only as a state with a word
beside it. There is one PageHead. Cinematic motion belongs only to the
opening, the bank-detail fix, "Why this number" and the sign-off stamp.

The owner asked for the score within minutes, so this pass covers the
hero path in depth and the second-level screens more lightly.

## Headline

The ink-and-grey palette has landed, and it is the biggest single
improvement since 7.3. No accent hue remains on any desktop screen I
measured. Red, amber and green appear only beside a word ("Stops pay",
"Check", "Ready to upload"). The panel glass is gone. Group-tab text now
aligns to the 152px column on most pages. The pay run has a compact
sticky header: title, four dots, the step name and one ink button. That
was upgrade 7 of the last critique, and it is the best structural change
in this round. The HR dashboard now opens with four tiles and agrees
with Fix ("2 stop pay, 6 to check").

It is still not a 10. Five things hold it back:

1. **Three title systems.**
   - Group pages: a 34/40 h1 at y179, under a 70px dead band below the
     tabs.
   - Payslip: 28px at y146.
   - Home: 28px inside the hero card at x184.
   - Pay run: the h1 is the 17px sticky title. The visible step title
     is a 22px h2 at y177.

   The "one PageHead" rule is not met.
2. **The type scale is not the token scale.** The measured sizes
   include 10, 14, 15.4, 15.84, 16.8, 19.04, 20.16 and 23.52px:
   - 14px: 91 runs on HR Home, 30+ on the payslip and 280 on Pay.
   - The fractional sizes come from em-based money parts.
3. **Engine language in the signature feature.** The explain drawer's
   "Rules used" shows "Overtime uplift: 2500", "Shift workers night
   excluded: true", "Rounding mode: half up" and "engine convention,
   not law". This was banned in the 26 Nov critique, and it now sits
   inside the cinematic "Why this number" moment.
4. **Dead and wrong states on the path.**
   - Bare `#/setup` and `#/setup/access` render "We could not find
     that." in browser-blue at x32, outside the grid.
   - The GM's stamp reads 00:27 for a run prepared at 09:30 the same
     day, so the sign-off appears to come before the preparation.
   - The Executive dashboard tile says "Before sign-off 0" over "6 to
     check".
   - Home says 4 decisions wait; the HR dashboard says 5.
5. **The phone employee Home is unchanged.**
   - A 22pt figure with "Jose Reyes" printed under his own pay.
   - A bordered "To sign: 3" card on its own padding.
   - The sign sheet uses the browser's blue radio buttons, the only
     accent hue left in the product.

**Overall: 7.9/10** (up from 7.3).

## 2. Per screen: the three most visible flaws and the fix

### Home, HR desktop (`#/home`), 8

1. **The h1 sits inside the hero card** (28/33.6 at x184, y147). Every
   other page has a 34/40 h1 at x152.
   - Fix: keep the statement as Home's PageHead, at the shared slot, x
     on the column. The card starts under it.
   - Where: `home/PayRunHero.tsx` and `ui/templates` PageHead.
2. **Dead areas.**
   - The left column ends at "5 more to review" while Decisions runs on
     about 230px further, leaving a hole.
   - The hero card has about 50px of empty space between the step
     tracker and the pension footnote.
   - The "Off in the next 7 days" tile is still a big 0 over seven grey
     dashes.
   - Fix: let "Before sign-off" take the full width when Decisions is
     longer, or cap Decisions at 3 with "All decisions".
   - Fix: when the count is 0, the tile says "Nobody is off this week"
     at 15/22 and shows the next absence as the main line.
   - Where: `home/PeopleStrip.tsx`.
3. **Off-scale type.**
   - 14px runs: 91, in Decisions and the cards.
   - 22/22 and 15.84px money parts.
   - The sparkline is still a flat grey slab.
   - Fix: snap to the 12/13/15/17/22/28/56 tokens. Money parts should
     use token sizes, not em fractions (`ui/Money.tsx`).
   - Fix: draw the sparkline as a line with no fill, on a y-axis fitted
     to the range plus 10%.

## 4. Verdict and the second design round, by impact

No screen is at 9. To get the hero path there, a second round must do
these, in order:

1. **One PageHead (M).** One component and one slot:
   - The h1 is 28/34 at x152, 40px under the bar, or 24px under the
     tab row; that kills the 70px band.
   - Home's statement and the payslip use it.
   - The pay run keeps its sticky row, but the step title becomes the
     PageHead.
   - First tab text on x152 everywhere.

   Files: `ui/templates` (PageHead), `shell/GroupTabs.tsx`,
   `home/PayRunHero.tsx`, `people/Payslip.tsx`.
2. **Explain drawer content (S).**
   - Parameters become sentences.
   - No green; ink plus a word.
   - Every step carries its value.
   - The drawer starts under the banner.

   Files: `components/explain`.
3. **Fix-step header honesty (S).** No live "Continue to Sign off"
   while anything stops pay. Show plain "Waiting for Khalid" for the
   preparer on Sign-off.
4. **Type tokens enforced (M).**
   - Remove the 14px usage and the em-fraction money parts.
   - Add a unit test like `tokens.test.ts` that fails on any computed
     size outside the scale.

   Files: `ui/Money.tsx`, `home/*`, `payrun/Pay*`, `theme/tokens.css`.
5. **Phone employee Home as a pass (M).**
   - A 48pt figure, no name.
   - One inset "To do" list.
   - One primary.
   - The same banner height on every role.
6. **Sign sheet (S).** Use `accent-color: ink`, a one-line 22pt title,
   the document in the text face, a folded fingerprint, one-row
   document cards and a short toast.
7. **Stamp time and approval counts (S).**
   - The demo clock drives every timestamp.
   - One `approvals` source for Home, the dashboards and Cmd K (4
     versus 5).
   - The Exec tile reads "6 to check", not "0".
8. **Setup route and NotFound (S).** Send `#/setup` to the first live
   area. Build `NotFound` on the empty-state template inside the grid.
9. **Dotted underline discipline and Review clean-up (S).**
   - Underline only derived figures.
   - Drop the coachmark, or show it after the roll.
   - A one-line pension note.
   - One footer rule.
10. **Charts that say something (M).**
    - Line sparklines on a fitted axis.
    - Month-over-month diverging bars for Trend and Exec.
    - A visible axis break on Variance and a true minus at figure size.
    - "Nobody is off" empty tiles.

Done well, items 1 to 3 and 5 to 7 put Home, Review, Fix, the payslip,
Sign-off and the phone Home at 9. Items 4, 8, 9 and 10 bring the rest
to about 8.5 to 9. Expected overall after the round: about 9.
