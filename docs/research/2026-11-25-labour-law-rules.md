# Labour law rules for the rule library (snippet-level)

Source texts to read in full before coding (all from official domains):

- Decree-Law 33/2021: uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541
- Executive Regulation (Cabinet Res. 1/2022): .../legislations/1547
- MOHRE overtime, wages and leave guide (mohre.gov.ae)

Status: OFFICIAL means an official-domain snippet; the page was not
opened. Article numbers are mostly unconfirmed.

| Rule | Value reported | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Normal hours | 8 a day or 48 a week (Art. 17) | official |
| Overtime | basic wage plus at least 25% | official |
| Night overtime 22:00 to 04:00 | basic plus at least 50%; not for shift workers | official |
| Overtime cap | 2 extra hours a day | official |
| Rest day or holiday work | day off in lieu, or pay plus at least 50% | official |
| Ramadan | 2 hours shorter | unconfirmed |
| Annual leave | 30 days a year; unused paid at basic wage | official |
| Sick leave | 90 days: 15 full, 30 half, rest unpaid | secondary |
| Maternity | 60 days: 45 full, 15 half (Art. 30) | secondary |
| Parental | 5 working days within 6 months | official |
| Bereavement | 5 days spouse, 3 days relatives | conflicting |
| Hajj | unpaid, up to 30 days, once | official |
| Deductions | total cap 50% of wage; overpayment recovery 20% | official (partial) |
| Notice | 30 to 90 days (Art. 43) | official |
| Probation | up to 6 months | official |
| Gratuity | Art. 51: 21 days, then 30, cap 2 years, basic wage | official (in engine) |
| Final dues deadline | 14 days after contract end | official |
| Unlawful termination | up to 3 months' wage | official |
| ILOE | AED 5 or 10 a month; fine AED 400 | fines official, rest secondary |

Gaps: pay in lieu of notice, resignation gratuity rules, the list of
allowed deduction grounds, the 5% versus five days penalty conflict.

Use: these become rule-pack parameters in later plans, each with its
status. Nothing here is coded in Plan 2.
