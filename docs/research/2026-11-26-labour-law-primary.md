Date: 2026-11-26. Purpose: article numbers and near-verbatim text.

## 1. Working hours (DL Art 17, Art 18; ER Art 15) READ

DL Art 17(1): "The maximum normal working hours for Workers shall be
eight (8) hours per day or forty-eight (48) hours per week."
DL Art 17(2): Cabinet may increase or decrease hours for sectors or
categories. Art 17(4): "The Executive Regulation ... shall determine
the working hours in the month of Ramadan."
DL Art 17(5): full-timer may not be required to work more than the
hours agreed in the contract, except with written consent.
DL Art 18: "may not work for more than five (5) consecutive hours
without a rest period or periods amounting in aggregate to not less
than one hour."
ER Art 15(4)(d): shift/round activities exempt from the max limit
"provided that the average of working hours does not exceed (56)
hours per week". Also exempt: board members, supervisors with
employer capacity, maritime crews (15(4)(a)-(c)).

## 2. Overtime, night work, rest-day work (DL Art 19) READ

Art 19(1): extra hours "do not exceed two hours per day" (beyond that
only per ER conditions). "In all cases, the total working hours shall
not exceed one hundred and forty-four (144) hours every (3) three
weeks."
Art 19(2): overtime pay = "a wage equal to that corresponding to his
normal working hours - calculated according to the basic wage - plus
an increase of at least twenty-five percent (25%) of such wage."
Art 19(3): overtime "between 10 pm and 4 am ... the wage determined
for normal working hours - calculated according to the basic wage -
plus an increase equal to at least fifty percent (50%) of such wage.
Shift Workers shall be excluded from the above."
Art 19(4): work on the contractual rest day: "compensated with
another rest day, or with payment of the wage for that day according
to the wage established for normal business days, plus an increase not
less than fifty percent (50%) of the basic wage for that day."
Art 19(5): no more than two consecutive rest days of work, excluding
day laborers.
Art 19(5), official English text (search excerpt of the
uaelegislation.gov.ae PDF, not read in full; added 2026-11-26 after
the Plan 9 engine review): "The Worker may not be required to work
for more than two consecutive rest days, excluding day laborers."
The day-laborer exclusion is not modelled by the engine.
Note: night work is only addressed as overtime between 10 pm and 4 am.
No separate night-shift premium for ordinary hours: NOT FOUND.
ER Art 15(3): extra hours beyond 2 per day allowed only to prevent
"gross loss or a serious accident"; 144 hours per 3 weeks still caps.
Art 20 (DL): categories exempt from hours limits set by the ER (ER
15(4) above).

## 3. Weekly rest and official holidays (DL Art 21, Art 28) READ

Art 21: "paid weekly rest not less than one day, according to ... the
employment contract or the work organizational regulation."
Art 28(1): "entitled to an official leave with full pay on the
official holidays to be determined by a Cabinet resolution."
Art 28(2): work on an official holiday: "another rest day for each
day ... or with payment of the wage for that day according to the wage
established for normal business days, plus an increase not less than
fifty percent (50%) of the basic wage for that day."
The list of official holidays is a Cabinet resolution: NOT FOUND here.

## 4. Wage definitions (DL Art 1) READ

"Basic Wage: The wage specified in the employment contract, which is
paid to the Worker in return for his work ... on a monthly, weekly,
daily, hourly or piece-meal basis, and does not include any other
allowances or benefits in kind."
"Wage: The basic wage, plus the cash allowances and benefits in kind
that are decided for the Worker under the employment contract or this
Decree by law" (may include in-kind benefits or cash equivalent,
risk/effort allowances, cost-of-living allowances, sales or profit
percentages).
Used by the law: overtime, rest-day and holiday uplifts and gratuity
use BASIC wage. Notice pay (Art 43), annual leave pay (Art 29(6)),
sick pay, maternity pay use "wage"/"last wage received".
DL Art 22(1)-(3): amount/type fixed in contract; wages paid in AED
unless another currency agreed in the contract.
DL Art 23: piece-rate daily wage = average over the 6 months before
the request or lawsuit. Art 27: Cabinet may set a minimum wage.
Art 26(1): employer must enable the worker to work "otherwise he is
obligated to pay his wage agreed upon."

## 5. Deductions (DL Art 25; Art 39(1)(c); Art 40; ER Art 29) READ

Art 25(1): "No amount of money shall be deducted from the Worker's
wage except in the following cases":
 a. recovering loans, "within the maximum monthly deduction rate",
    after the worker's written consent, "without any interests".
 b. "amounts paid to the Worker in excess of his entitlements,
    provided that the amount deducted shall not exceed twenty percent
    (20%) of the wage."
 c. contributions to bonuses, pensions, insurances under State law.
 d. savings fund contributions or fund loans approved by the Ministry.
 e. instalments of welfare schemes or benefits approved by the
    Ministry, with the worker's written agreement.
 f. violations under the Ministry-approved sanction regulation,
    "provided that it shall not exceed five percent (5%) of the wage."
 g. court-judgment debts, "not exceed a quarter of the wage", except
    alimony (more than a quarter allowed); several debts are paid by
    privilege rank.
 h. damage repair (fault or breach of instructions causing loss of
    tools, machines, products, materials): "shall not exceed the wage
    of (5) five days per month, and no more amounts shall be deducted
    except upon the approval of the competent court."
Art 25(2): "If there are multiple reasons for deduction from the wage,
it is not permissible in all cases for the percentage of deduction to
exceed fifty percent (50%) of the wage."
DISCREPANCY: Art 39(1)(c) sanction list says "Deduction from the wage
not exceeding the wages of five (5) days per month", whereas Art 25(1)(f)
says 5% of wage. Both are in the primary text. Needs a legal ruling;
5% is the stricter reading for violation deductions.
Art 39(1) also lists: written caution; written warning; suspension up to
14 days without pay; no periodic raise up to 1 year; no promotion up to
2 years; dismissal with gratuity preserved.
Art 40(1): suspension for investigation up to 30 days "with a suspension
of half the wage"; paid back if reserved, no violation or warning only.
Art 40(2): suspension on criminal accusation: wage suspended; full
back pay if released or acquitted for non-felony.
Art 34: no wage for absence after leave ends without legitimate reason.
ER Art 29(1): deductions from end-of-service pay (loans, overpayments,
pension/insurance deductions, sanction amounts, court debts, damage).
ER Art 29(2): violation/damage deductions only if "a period of (3)
three months has not lapsed on the amounts owed starting from the date
they became due - unless otherwise is agreed."
Whether the 50% cap applies to final settlement: NOT FOUND.

## 6. Annual leave (DL Art 29; ER Art 18, Art 19) READ

Art 29(1): "annual leave with full pay of not less than: a. Thirty days
for each year of his extended service. b. Two days for each month if
his service period is more than six months and less than one year.
c. A leave for parts of the last year he spent at work, in the event
that his service ends before using his annual leave balance."
Art 29(3): leave may be granted in probation; right to compensation for
the remainder is retained if probation is not passed.
Art 29(4): must be enjoyed in the year of entitlement; employer sets
dates, one month's notice.
Art 29(5) (printed as "1." in the English PDF): carry forward with
employer approval and organizational regulations.
Art 29(6): "The Worker is entitled to the wage for the period of his
annual leave." Art 29(7): rest days inside an interrupted leave count
as leave unless more beneficial terms exist.
Art 29(8): employer may not prevent leave for more than two years
unless the worker wants to carry it forward or take cash.
Art 29(9): "entitled to receive wages for the days of the entitled rest
days if he leaves the work before using it, regardless of their
duration."
ER Art 19(1): "may carry forward, into the following year, what does not
exceed half of the annual leave or agree with the employer to receive
its equivalent in cash in accordance with the wage he earns at the time
the leave is due."
ER Art 19(2): on termination "entitled to receive a cash equivalent of
the balance of his annual leaves due by law in accordance with his
basic salary."
Pay base therefore: in-service cash-out uses wage at time leave is
due; end-of-service payout uses BASIC salary (ER 19(2)).
ER Art 18: part-time leave pro rata by hours, minimum five working days
per annum.

## 7. Sick leave (DL Art 31; ER Art 20) READ

Art 31(1): notify employer within 3 business days; medical report.
Art 31(2): no paid sick leave during probation (unpaid leave possible).
Art 31(3): after probation, "no more than (90) ninety consecutive or
intermittent days per year ... a. The first fifteen (15) days with full
pay. b. The following thirty (30) days with half pay. c. The following
period shall be unpaid."
Art 31(4): not paid if illness results from misconduct (cases in ER).
Art 31(5): employer may terminate after sick leave is exhausted, paying
all financial dues. Art 46: no termination for medical unfitness before
the worker has used leaves legally due.
ER Art 20: no sick leave for illness caused by alcohol or drugs
(authority report required) or violating safety instructions.

## 8. Maternity, parental, other paid leave (DL Art 30, 32, 33; ER 21) READ

Art 30(1): "maternity leave of (60) sixty days ... a. The first
forty-five (45) days with full pay. b. The following fifteen (15) days
with half pay."
Art 30(2): up to 45 further days unpaid for illness of mother or child
from pregnancy/childbirth (medical certificate); not counted for
gratuity or pension service.
Art 30(3): applies if birth after 6 months or more of pregnancy, live
or stillborn.
Art 30(4): sick or disabled child needing a companion: 30 days full pay
after maternity leave plus 30 days unpaid extension.
Art 30(5): leave may start from the last day of the month before the
expected birth month. Art 30(7): if she works for another employer
during the leave, original employer may withhold or recover the wage.
Art 30(8): no termination or notice because of pregnancy or leave.
Art 30(9): up to six months after birth, one or two breastfeeding
breaks per day totalling no more than one hour.
Art 32(1)(a): mourning leave "five (5) days" for spouse; "Three (3)
days" for mother, father, son, brother, sister, grandson, grandfather,
grandmother, from the date of death.
Art 32(1)(b): parental leave "(5) five working days" for either
parent, continuous or intermittent, within 6 months of birth.
Art 32(2): study leave 10 business days per year (service 2+ years).
Art 32(3): national service leave with pay. Art 33: unpaid leave by
agreement; excluded from service period and pension contribution.
ER Art 21(5): mourning, parental, annual and unpaid leave may be
combined.

## 9. Notice period and pay in lieu (DL Art 43, Art 44, Art 9) READ

Art 43(1): written notice; period agreed in contract, "not less than
thirty (30) days, and not more than ninety (90) days."
Art 43(2): "entitled to his full wage for that period according to the
last wage received"; same period for both parties unless better for
the worker; may be waived or reduced by agreement keeping the worker's
rights.
Art 43(3): party in breach pays "a compensation called a warning
allowance ... equal to the Worker's wage for the entire warning period
or the remaining part of it."
Art 43(4): based on the last wage received (month, week, day, hour) or
the average daily wage (piece-rate).
Art 43(5): if employer terminates, worker may be absent one UNPAID
working day per week to job-hunt, with 3 days' notice.
Art 35: notice during leave (heading read; body not read closely).
Art 44 lists 10 grounds for dismissal without notice (written
investigation, written reasoned decision); Art 45 lists worker exits
without notice; Art 47(2): unlawful dismissal compensation capped at 3
months of last wage, on top of notice pay and gratuity (47(3)).

## 10. Probation (DL Art 9) READ

Art 9(1): "not exceeding six (6) months"; employer may terminate "after
notifying the Worker in writing at least fourteen (14) days prior".
Art 9(2): only once per employer; counts toward service.
Art 9(5): breach of Art 9 requires "compensation equal to the Worker's
wage for the notice period or the remaining period of the notice
period."
Art 9(4)/(6): foreign worker leaving the State: 14 days' notice; one-year
work-permit ban if rules not followed. Art 9(3): moving to another
employer in the State needs one month's written notice; new employer
compensates recruitment costs unless agreed otherwise.
Sick leave pay not available in probation (Art 31(2)).

## 11. End-of-service gratuity (DL Art 51, 52; ER Art 29, 30) READ

Art 51(1): nationals: per pension and social security legislation.
Art 51(2): foreign full-time worker "who has completed one or more
years of continuous service ... calculated according to the basic
wage, as follows: a. The wage of twenty-one (21) days for each year of
the first five years of service. b. The wage of thirty (30) days for
each year in excess of that."
Art 51(3): "a bonus for parts of the year in proportion to the amount
spent in work, provided that he has completed one year of continuous
service."
Art 51(4): "days of absence from work without pay shall not be included
in the calculation of the duration of service."
Art 51(5): calculated on "the last basic wage received" (month/week/
day) or the average daily wage (piece-rate).
Art 51(6): "total end-of-service gratuity ... does not exceed two years'
wage."
Art 51(7): employer may deduct amounts due by law or judgment, per ER.
Art 51(8): Cabinet may approve alternative gratuity schemes.
Resignation rules: NOT FOUND. Art 51 has no reduction tied to
resignation or termination type (the word "resign" does not appear
anywhere in the DL text read). The old 1980 law's resignation
fractions are not in this text (Art 73 repeals Law 8 of 1980).
Art 39(1)(g) and Art 44 dismissal: gratuity preserved for sanction
dismissal (39(1)(g) explicit). Art 44 text on gratuity for summary
dismissal: no loss stated in the part read.
Art 52 and ER Art 30: part-time gratuity = (annual contract hours /
annual full-time hours) x full-time gratuity; none for temporary
employment or under one year.
Art 30(2) (maternity unpaid extension) excluded from gratuity service.

## 12. Final settlement and wage payment timing READ / partial

DL Art 53: "The Employer shall pay the Worker, within fourteen (14)
days as of the date of the end of the contract, his wages and all other
entitlements stipulated in this Decree by law and the resolutions
issued in implementation thereof, the contract or the Establishment's
Articles of Association." READ.
DL Art 22(2): wages paid "on their due dates in accordance with the
systems approved in the Ministry" (no day count). ER Art 16(1):
paid on due dates as per contract and Ministry standards; "through the
Wage Protection System or any other system approved by the Ministry."
READ.
Specific monthly pay deadline (e.g. within 15 days of month end) and
the WPS delay penalties: NOT FOUND in DL or ER; they sit in Ministry
circulars, unread.
ER Art 36: in Cabinet-declared emergencies, wage reduction, paid or
unpaid leave, remote work may be applied (READ).

## 13. Ramadan hours (ER Art 15(2)) READ

"The normal working hours shall be reduced by two hours during the holy
month of Ramadan." Enabled by DL Art 17(4). Applies to normal hours;
whether pay changes: nothing says wages fall, so full wage applies
(inference, not text). Overtime in Ramadan is then hours beyond the
reduced day (inference).
