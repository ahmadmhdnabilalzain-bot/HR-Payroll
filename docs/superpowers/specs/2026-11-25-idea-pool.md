# Idea Pool (raw, round 2)

Proposals only. All are bets. "Found" means a vendor page or search snippet
says so; "not found" means not found in that agent's searches, never "nobody
has it". "Needs partner" means a licensed provider is required. Overlaps with
the six accepted candidates are marked. The master list merges and ranks
these once all idea-finders have reported.

## Batch 1 (reported): five lenses

### G. Global leaders versus UAE pages
- G1 Signed agent actions: the customer's own AI drafts a pay run, leave
  change or approval; nothing commits until a named human confirms a diff,
  with the engine trace attached. Gusto and Zoho have connectors; the edge
  is the trace and one-model breadth.
- G2 Explain-my-payslip agent: the agent form of accepted candidate 4.
  Workday has a payslip explanation skill; no UAE vendor found.
- G3 Pre-run variance agent that names the cause of each variance (joiner,
  leave, rule change). ADP and Workday flag variances; cause attribution is
  the edge.
- G4 Policy agent on payroll data: auto-approves low-risk expense claims,
  flags duplicates, posts reimbursements into the pay run. Ramp has agents
  that are not payroll-aware. Cards need partner.
- G5 Rule-change impact and approval: affected employees, AED change per
  payslip, draft staff notice, then approval. Overlaps accepted candidate 1.
- G6 Offer-to-net what-if with a verified, shareable link. Overlaps accepted
  candidate 3. Deel and Gusto calculators are generic.
- G7 Agent for accountants: one AI conversation across client companies with
  per-client permissions. Gusto Pro has a multi-client connector; no UAE
  equivalent found.
- G8 Earned-wage access capped by the engine's projected net pay. Needs
  partner.

### A. Governed AI
- A1 Cited-answer contract: every answer built only from trace and rule
  entries, with rule id, version and source article; refuses when no rule
  matches; a test fails the build on any uncited number.
- A2 Correction proposals with a trace diff: drafts a change to inputs (never
  to pay), shows before and after, owner confirms.
- A3 Bound action receipts: approvals bind to exact parameters with expiry,
  policy version and rule version, plus a hashed execution receipt.
- A4 Scoped connector layer: read-only default, per-user scopes, writes only
  emit proposals; one connector across HR, expenses, AP and projects.
- A5 Public accuracy scoreboard: a versioned golden-case suite with pass rates
  per release that customers can rerun (Remote has a global AI payroll
  benchmark; no UAE rule-level one found).
- A6 Document intake with field provenance (Emirates ID, visa, contract,
  invoice, receipt), cross-checked against records and rules, human confirms
  before writing. Bayzat has OCR.
- A7 Permission-aware natural-language reports that compile to an inspectable
  saved query with definitions. Bayzat and ZenHR have AI reporting.
- A8 Injection-safe ingestion: uploaded documents and emails treated as
  untrusted; extraction has no tool access; red-team tests in the scoreboard.

### U. One data model across modules
- U1 Settle everything at exit: gratuity, leave pay, notice, open claims,
  card balance, loans, tickets and tasks in one statement, each deduction
  tied to a legal basis with a "cannot lawfully deduct" flag. Risk: deductions
  from gratuity are legally narrow (Article 51, clause 7). Card part needs
  partner.
- U2 True cost ledger per employee and project from the engine's own
  accruals (gratuity, leave pay, visa and ID fees, tickets, spend). Project
  costing exists at Keka; the UAE accrual-based version is the angle.
- U3 Payroll-aware cash-flow forecast: exact WPS date and amount from the
  draft run, plus payables, with what-if hires and leavers.
- U4 Leave-aware and budget-aware approval routing (reroute when approver or
  requester is on leave, resigning, on probation, or the budget is spent).
- U5 Pay-run preflight across spend: open claims, unreconciled card spend,
  over-limit items, lawful deductions, with pay, recover-later or hold.
- U6 Project margin forecast that includes planned leave, holidays and
  Ramadan hours. Keka PSA covers project P&L only.
- U7 Card limits that follow employment state (probation, notice, leave,
  salary-advance cap). Needs partner. Bare card freeze on exit exists
  (Pleo, Ramp, Rippling, Rho).
- U8 Offboarding task sweep that ends in the settlement and clearance letter.
  Keka and Bayzat already bundle parts.

### C. Complaints turned into features
- C1 Contract-sync gate: each payroll line carries the "contract on file with
  MOHRE" field; salary revisions create an amendment to-do; the run is
  flagged until they match. A vendor guide cites a case with an AED 40,000
  fine for a salary-contract mismatch (unverified).
- C2 Ticket with trace: "ask about this line" opens a ticket with trace, rule
  version and inputs attached, plus a drafted answer.
- C3 Error-remedy ledger: public dated correction records, affected runs and
  employees listed, corrected delta file, written remedy policy.
- C4 Run payroll by entity, project or nationality batch with cost landing in
  project billing. (Zoho's "no batch runs" was not confirmed.)
- C5 Pay-structure sandbox: test formulas against sample employees with
  trace and legal-conflict warnings, versioned and reversible.
- C6 Ask-the-report: plain-words reports that show definition and source
  rows, drill to the trace.
- C7 Expense claim evidence checklist per policy at submit time.
- C8 Pre-pay gate across leave, loans, advances, claims and unapproved changes.

### W. UAE workforce reality
- W1 WhatsApp-native self-service and approvals, same engine and audit trail
  (WhatsApp flows exist at Zoho People and others; no UAE-specific payroll
  one found). Owner "approve payroll by phone" folds in here.
- W2 Worker-language pay: payslip explanation in Urdu, Hindi, Bengali,
  Malayalam, Tagalog, Nepali and more, with a short voice note per line.
- W3 Voice-first self-service for low-literacy workers.
- W4 Live end-of-service balance visible to the employee, citing the rule.
- W5 Visa and Emirates ID case tracker (owner and PRO checklist, reminders,
  cost forecast, blocked-payroll warning). No tracker product found.
- W6 Bureau console for accountants, PRO firms and payroll bureaus (billing,
  white label, client sign-off).
- W7 Camp and deduction ledger: accommodation and transport charges, the 50%
  deduction cap, record-keeping for the MoHRE accommodation rules
  (Resolution 122 of 2026, per a law-firm summary; verify).
- W8 Pay-proof pack and cash-out: dated bundle of payslip, WPS reference and
  bank evidence; salary cards, earned-wage access and remittance hand-off.
  Needs partner.
